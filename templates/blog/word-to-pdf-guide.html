How to convert Word to PDF

Word documents are frequently shared in PDF format because PDF files maintain formatting across devices.

  1. Open the Word document you want to convert.
  2. Upload it to SecureConvert.
  3. Click the conversion button.
  4. Download the converted PDF.

This is a simple way to share formal documents, reports, and assignments.
