Privacy Policy

SecureConvert is designed with privacy first. We do not require account creation, we do not sell user data, and we do not build personal user profiles.

What we do

  • Accept a file upload for conversion
  • Convert it to PDF
  • Return the PDF to the user
  • Delete temporary files after processing

What we do not do

  • Store user documents
  • Create user accounts
  • Track browsing behavior
  • Sell personal data
  • Use invasive ad trackers

Temporary files are cleaned up automatically after conversion.
